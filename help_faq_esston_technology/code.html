The Esston motor starter allows you to start and stop your agricultural motor remotely using SMS commands or through the mobile app. It provides overload protection, dry run protection and easy installation.
Esston offers models tailored for diverse agricultural needs:
  • Esston Eco 1-Phase: For 1 HP to 3 HP open-well pumps.
  • Esston Pro 3-Phase: Heavy duty for 3 HP to 15 HP submersible borewells.
  • Esston Solar Hybrid: Works seamlessly with solar irrigation inverters.
1. Mount the waterproof IP65 casing away from direct rainwater spray.
2. Connect incoming 3-phase lines to L1-L2-L3 input terminals.
3. Connect pump wires to motor load terminals.
4. Mount the magnetic antenna externally facing open sky for optimal GSM reception.

START <motor> - Turn motor ON

STOP <motor> - Turn motor OFF

STATUS - Get live voltage & phase health

TIMER 60 - Run pump for 60 minutes only

Navigate to the Motor Control tab in this app, choose your active starter unit, and tap "Add Schedule". You can configure days of the week, start times, and auto-cutoff duration according to electricity tariff availability.
Includes auto Dry Run cut-off, Single Phasing prevention, High/Low voltage shutdown (under 300V or over 460V), and thermal motor overload coil protection.
